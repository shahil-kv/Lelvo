Enjoying Memvo?

Memvo is free and open source, and it stays that way. There's no paid plan, no ads and nothing locked. That also means the only thing that keeps it going is support from the people who use it. If Memvo saves you a storage plan, consider giving a little of that back.

Where your support goes

Apple's yearly fee $99 a year
Apple charges every developer to publish on the App Store and TestFlight. Without it, Memvo can't be installed the normal way.
Devices for testing
iPhones and Macs on different versions of iOS and macOS, so an update never breaks someone's backup.
Time
Memvo is built by one developer, in evenings and weekends: new features, keeping up with Telegram and Apple changes, fixing bugs and answering your questions.
Not servers
Memvo has none, and your photos live in your own Telegram. Nothing you give goes to hosting; it all goes into the app.
