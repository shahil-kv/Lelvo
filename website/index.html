Why Lelvo

Stop renting space for your own memories.

Storage plans grow with your library, and so does the bill. Lelvo keeps your originals in the Telegram account you already have, encrypted so only you can open them.

Features

Feels like Photos, with a backup you own.

One timelineSync

Everything in one place

Photos on your phone and everything in your backup, together by day, month or year. Back up all of it with one tap, or just the ones you pick.

The small mark on each photo shows where it lives: green for backed up, a cloud for only in your backup.

CollectionsOn device

Memories, albums and search

Trips, recent days, your albums and a map. Search for places, months, albums and kinds of photos.

All of it is worked out on your device. Nothing is sent anywhere to be analysed.

ViewerStreaming

Opens at once from the cloud

Big photos show a screen copy straight away, and videos play in 1080p and seek instantly, even on mobile data. One tap gets the original.

The 4K original stays untouched for saving and sharing. Nothing fills up your phone.

RestoreBit for bit

Comes back exactly as it was

Sign in on a new phone and your library is there, with dates, places, edits, RAW files, Live Photos, favourites and albums.

Save to Photos puts back the original with its edit, and the RAW of RAW + JPEG pairs.

Free up spaceVerified

Free up space, safely

A photo counts as backed up only after Telegram confirms every part. Only those are offered for removal, and your phone asks you to confirm.

Delete something from your backup and it stays in Recently Deleted for 30 days.

Security

Telegram keeps the files. Only you hold the key.

Lelvo locks every photo, video and its details on your device before upload. Telegram stores only scrambled files, and only your passphrase opens them.

Details

Made for a lifetime of photos.

The things you only notice when a backup gets them wrong.

Originals, bit for bit
HEIC, ProRAW, ProRes, 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision, with the file name, date and place intact.
Never twice
Every photo is matched by its fingerprint, so nothing uploads twice, even after a reinstall or from another device.
Backs up on its own
New photos go up without opening Lelvo: within a minute on a Mac, and in the background on iPhone and Android.
Files of any size
Up to 2 GB in one go, 4 GB with Telegram Premium. Bigger videos are split into parts and joined again when you open them.
Bring your Google Photos
Import a Google Takeout and keep each photo's date, place, albums and favourites.
A second copy, if you want
On a Mac, pick an external drive or a NAS once, and Lelvo keeps a copy there in Year and Month folders.

How it works

Set up in a minute.

  1. Open Lelvo

    Tap Continue with Telegram. There's no Lelvo account to make.

  2. Sign in with Telegram

    Your phone number, then the code Telegram sends to your other Telegram apps.

  3. Choose and back up

    Set your passphrase, keep your recovery key, pick what to include, then tap Back up now.

Questions

Before you trust it with your photos.

Plain answers, with links to Telegram's own pages where Telegram sets the rules. More in the full FAQ.

Can Telegram see my photos?

No. Every Lelvo library is end-to-end encrypted. Telegram cloud chats on their own aren't (Telegram FAQ), so Lelvo encrypts every file and its details on your device first, and Telegram only ever holds files it can't open.

What should I do before trusting it with my only copy?

Keep your passphrase and recovery key somewhere safe, because nobody can recover them for you. Open Telegram now and then, since Telegram deletes accounts that stay inactive for a long time (Telegram FAQ). Don't delete the library channel by hand. For photos you can't replace, keep a second copy on a drive too: Lelvo on a Mac can do that for you.

Is the storage really unlimited?

Telegram describes its cloud storage as unlimited, with files up to 2 GB each, or 4 GB with Telegram Premium (Terms of Service, FAQ). That's Telegram's offer, not a promise Lelvo can make, and Telegram can change it. Lelvo splits bigger files into parts on its own.

Is this allowed by Telegram?

Yes. Telegram opens its API to third-party apps under the Telegram API Terms of Service. Lelvo follows them: it uses its own app key, says clearly that it uses the Telegram API, doesn't put "Telegram" in its name or use Telegram's logo, and doesn't change how Telegram works for anyone else.

Can my account get banned for this?

Backing up your own photos to your own private channel is ordinary use. Bans come from breaking Telegram's Terms of Service, such as spam or scams. Lelvo only posts to your private channel, uploads at most two files at a time with a pause between them, and waits whenever Telegram asks it to slow down.

How do I restore on a new phone?

Install Lelvo, sign in with the same Telegram account and pick your library. Dates, places, albums and Live Photos come back, and photos already on the phone aren't uploaded again.

What if I delete the channel, or messages in it?

A deleted channel takes every file in it, and it can't be restored by Lelvo or anyone else. Photos still on your phone are safe: after a re-scan they show as not backed up, and Retry uploads them again. Please don't delete or edit messages in the library channel by hand.

Can Lelvo delete photos from my phone?

Only when you ask, and iOS always asks you to confirm. Free up space offers only photos that Lelvo has just checked are complete in Telegram and unchanged since their backup.

Is it really free? Who pays for it?

Lelvo is free and open source under the GPL-3.0, and every feature stays free. Your storage is your own Telegram account's, so there's no plan to buy. Making Lelvo isn't free, though: the Apple developer membership costs $99 a year, plus test devices, the website and a lot of evenings. If Lelvo saves you a storage plan, a small contribution keeps it going.
