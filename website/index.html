How it works Questions GitHub

Your photos, kept in your own Telegram.

Memvo backs up every photo and video on your iPhone and Mac, untouched, to a private channel in your Telegram account. Browse everything in one timeline, stream videos without downloading, and get it all back on a new phone by signing in.

Download for iPhone and Mac Read the source code

Free and open source. iOS 17 and macOS 15 or later. TestFlight and the App Store are coming.
Memvo uses the Telegram API. It isn't made or endorsed by Telegram.

How it works

  1. Sign in with your Telegram account

    Enter your phone number and the code Telegram sends you, like any Telegram app. No new account, no password to remember.

  2. Memvo makes a private channel

    Your library lives there, as normal files you own. Choose Standard, or end-to-end encrypted with a passphrase only you know.

  3. Back up, browse, restore

    Tap Back up now. Photos on your phone and in the cloud show up in one timeline. On a new phone, sign in and everything comes back.

What it does

Originals, bit for bit
HEIC, ProRAW, ProRes, 4K, HDR and Dolby Vision, with EXIF and location intact. Edited photos keep both the original and the edit.
Any size
Files up to 2 GB go up directly, 4 GB with Telegram Premium. Anything bigger is split into parts and joined again when you open it.
Streaming video
Backed-up videos start in a second or two straight from Telegram, with seeking, Picture in Picture and AirPlay.
Never twice
Every photo is matched by its Photos ID and checksum, so nothing uploads twice, even after a reinstall or on another device.
Optional encryption
AES-256 on your device before anything leaves it. Telegram stores only files it can't read.
Free up space, safely
Only photos that Memvo has just checked are complete in Telegram are offered for removal from your phone.
Never locked in
Download a copy of your whole library to Files, a drive or a Mac folder at any time. The storage format is open and documented.
iPhone and Mac
The same library on both, with a Mac app that has its own sidebar, keyboard shortcuts and menu bar status.

Nobody in the middle

Memvo has no servers and no accounts of its own. Your photos go from your device to your Telegram account and nowhere else. There's no analytics, no ads and no tracking, and the code that proves it is public. Your Telegram session and keys stay in your device's Keychain.

Privacy policy

Questions people ask

Most of them are about Telegram itself. The answers link to Telegram's own pages, because Telegram's rules are Telegram's to set.

Telegram and the rules

Is this allowed by Telegram?

Yes. Telegram opens its API to third-party apps under the Telegram API Terms of Service. Memvo follows them: it uses its own app key, says clearly that it uses the Telegram API, doesn't put "Telegram" in its name or use Telegram's logo, and doesn't change how Telegram works for anyone else.

Is the storage really unlimited?

Telegram describes its cloud storage as unlimited, with files up to 2 GB each, or 4 GB with Telegram Premium (Terms of Service, FAQ). That's Telegram's offer, not a promise Memvo can make, and Telegram can change it. Memvo splits bigger files into parts on its own.

Can my account get banned for this?

Backing up your own photos to your own private channel is ordinary use. Bans come from breaking Telegram's Terms of Service, such as spam or scams. Memvo only posts to your private channel, uploads at most two files at a time with a pause between them, and waits whenever Telegram asks it to slow down.

What does "Telegram asked to slow down" mean?

Telegram limits how fast any account can send. When it asks Memvo to wait, Memvo waits exactly that long and then carries on by itself. Nothing is lost, and respecting the limit is what keeps your account in good standing.

What happens if Telegram deletes my account for inactivity?

Telegram deletes accounts that don't come online for a set time, 18 months by default, and everything in them goes too (Telegram FAQ). Memvo shows your current setting and can change it to the longest Telegram allows, two years. Opening the Telegram app now and then keeps the account active.

What if my account gets frozen?

Telegram freezes accounts that were restricted after reports from other people. A frozen account can still read its chats but can't post, and you can appeal in the Telegram app (Telegram FAQ). In Memvo you can still browse, view and download your library; backups pause with a clear message and continue once the account is unfrozen.

My account is limited and can only message contacts. Does that stop backups?

That limit is Telegram's anti-spam rule for messaging people who don't have your number. Telegram suggests contacting @SpamBot if you think it's a mistake, and says first limits usually last a few days (Telegram spam FAQ). If Telegram also blocks creating a channel, Memvo tells you and suggests using a channel you already have.

What if I delete the channel, or messages in it?

A deleted channel takes every file in it, and it can't be restored by Memvo or anyone else. Photos still on your phone are safe: after a re-scan they show as not backed up, and Retry uploads them again. Please don't delete or edit messages in the library channel by hand.

Privacy and safety

Can Telegram see my photos?

In a Standard library, files are encrypted on the way and stored like any Telegram cloud chat, which isn't end-to-end encrypted (Telegram FAQ). In an End-to-end encrypted library, Memvo encrypts every file and its details on your device first, so Telegram only ever holds files it can't open.

I forgot my encryption passphrase.

Nobody can recover it, including the people who make Memvo. That's what end-to-end encryption means. Keep the passphrase in a password manager.

Does Memvo collect anything about me?

No. There are no Memvo servers, no analytics, no crash reporting and no ads. Your phone number and login code go only to Telegram, to sign you in.

Can Memvo delete photos from my phone?

Only when you ask, and iOS always asks you to confirm. Free up space offers only photos that Memvo has just checked are complete in Telegram and unchanged since their backup.

Using it

Does it log me out of my other Telegram apps?

No. Memvo is one more session, like Telegram Desktop. You'll see it in Telegram under Settings → Devices.

The login code doesn't arrive.

Telegram sends it to your other Telegram apps first, in the chat named Telegram, not by SMS. If you have no other device signed in, wait for the option to get it by SMS or a call.

Why does a big backup need the app open?

iPhones only let apps work in the background in short, system-controlled windows. Memvo uploads while it's open, keeps going in the background on iOS 26 and later after you tap Back up now, and also runs overnight while charging. It always continues where it stopped.

Will it fill up my phone?

No. Thumbnails and items you've viewed are cached with limits you choose, 300 MB and 1 GB by default, and you can clear them any time.

How do I restore on a new phone?

Install Memvo, sign in with the same Telegram account and pick your library. Dates, places, albums and Live Photos come back, and photos already on the phone aren't uploaded again.

How do I install it today?

Download the latest build from GitHub Releases. On iPhone, install it with SideStore, AltStore or Sideloadly; apps signed with a free Apple ID need refreshing every 7 days, which SideStore and AltStore do for you. A TestFlight beta and the App Store come next.

Is there an Android version?

Not planned. The project focuses on making the iPhone and Mac apps excellent. The storage format is documented, so anyone can build a compatible app.

Is it really free? Who pays for it?

It's free and open source under the GPL-3.0, and there's nothing to pay for: Memvo has no servers, and your storage is your Telegram account's. It's built by volunteers.
