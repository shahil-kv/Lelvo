Privacy policy

Effective 28 November 2026. Applies to the Lelvo apps for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android.

In one sentence: Lelvo sends your photos from your device to your own Telegram account and nowhere else; the people who make it never receive any of your data.

Who we are

Lelvo is a free, open-source app maintained by @shahil-kv and contributors. Contact: support@lelvo.app, or an issue at github.com/shahil-kv/Lelvo. Security problems: SECURITY.md.

What we collect

Nothing. Lelvo has no servers. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and it doesn't share data with anyone. We can't see who uses the app, your photos, your Telegram account or your library.

Why the store labels list data: the App Store privacy label and Google Play's Data safety section count anything an app's built-in code sends to another company. Lelvo uses Telegram's code to reach your account, so both list your phone number, photos and videos, and Telegram user ID. They're used only to run the app, never to track you or for ads, and they go only to Telegram, over an encrypted connection. We never receive them, and nothing is sold.

The website's waiting list

If you join the waiting list on the website, your email address and the device you chose are stored in a Google Sheet that only the maintainer can open, with the date you joined. They are used once, to tell you when Lelvo is on TestFlight and the App Store, and never shared or sold. To be removed, email support@lelvo.app. Google's privacy policy applies to the Sheet. The apps never collect your email.

Where your data goes

  • Photos and videos you back up go directly from your device to a private channel in your own Telegram account, over Telegram's encrypted connection. Telegram's privacy policy applies to what is stored there.
  • Your phone number, login code and two-step password go only to Telegram, to sign you in.
  • End-to-end encryption: every new library is encrypted on your device before upload, files and their details. Your passphrase and the key derived from it never leave your device, and nobody, including us, can recover them.
  • Links you open from the app (this policy, the FAQ, GitHub) open in your browser, under that site's own policy.

Places, maps and things in photos

These features use services built into your phone. None of them receives a photo, and none of them receives anything from us.

  • Place names (Search, Memories, the map): the app asks for the name of an area about 1 km across: Apple's service on iPhone, iPad and Mac, Google's on Android. It sends only the middle of that area, never a photo or its exact location. Apple's privacy policy or Google's privacy policy applies.
  • Maps: map pictures come from Apple Maps on iPhone, iPad and Mac, and from OpenFreeMap on Android. Like any map, the service sees which part of the world is on screen, and your IP address.
  • Things in photos ("dog", "beach"): read on your device. On iPhone, iPad and Mac by Apple's Vision, which sends nothing. On Android by Google's ML Kit, which runs on the phone: your photos and what it finds never leave it. ML Kit normally also sends Google usage statistics; Lelvo removes that part from the Android app, so ML Kit sends nothing. See Google's ML Kit terms.

What stays on your device

  • Your Telegram session (in an encrypted database), the Telegram app keys and your encryption key, in the Keychain on Apple devices and the Android Keystore on Android, readable only by Lelvo on this device and never synced.
  • A local index of your library (names, dates, sizes, places and Telegram message IDs) so the timeline opens quickly. It is excluded from device backups because it can be rebuilt from Telegram.
  • Caches of thumbnails and items you viewed, with size limits you control.
  • A diagnostic log, which never leaves the device unless you share it yourself. Phone numbers, keys, channel IDs and file IDs are removed from it.

Settings → Sign out and reset deletes all of this from the device, and so does deleting the app. It doesn't delete your library in Telegram.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • On your device: data stays until you sign out and reset, or delete the app.
  • In Telegram: your library stays in your channel until you delete it. Telegram keeps it under its own privacy policy, and we can't see or delete it for you. You can delete the channel in the Telegram app, or your whole account at my.telegram.org/delete.
  • To stop sharing: signing out ends Lelvo's access to your Telegram account. You can also end the session in Telegram under Settings → Devices. Turning off Photos access in your phone's or Mac's settings stops new backups.

Telegram is the only company that receives your photos, and only because you sign in and choose to back up. Apart from the place and map requests above, we don't share data with anyone, so no other company has to protect it.

Lelvo has no ads, no in-app purchases and no payments of any kind.

Permissions

  • Photos: to read the photos you choose to back up and to save downloaded ones back. You can allow limited access, or none, and still browse your cloud library.
  • Network: to talk to Telegram.
  • iPhone, if you turn it on: notifications, to tell you when a backup finished or is waiting. They are made on your iPhone and show only counts.
  • Android only: location in photos (to read where a photo was taken, for Places), notifications (to show a long backup's progress), and running in the background (for automatic backups you turn on).

Children

Lelvo isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly handle any child's data, because it handles no one's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date, and the history is visible in this repository.
