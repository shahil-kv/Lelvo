Privacy policy

Effective 24 October 2026. Applies to the Memvo apps for iPhone, iPad and Mac.

In one sentence: Memvo sends your photos from your device to your own Telegram account and nowhere else; the people who make it never receive any of your data.

Who we are

Memvo is a free, open-source app maintained by @shahil-kv and contributors. Contact: mshahilkv@gmail.com, or an issue at github.com/shahil-kv/Memvo. Security problems: SECURITY.md.

What we collect

Nothing. Memvo has no servers. It contains no analytics, advertising, tracking or crash-reporting code, and it doesn't share data with anyone. We can't see who uses the app, your photos, your Telegram account or your library.

Where your data goes

  • Photos and videos you back up go directly from your device to a private channel in your own Telegram account, over Telegram's encrypted connection. Telegram's privacy policy applies to what is stored there.
  • Your phone number, login code and two-step password go only to Telegram, to sign you in.
  • End-to-end encrypted libraries: files and their details are encrypted on your device before upload. Your passphrase and the key derived from it never leave your device, and nobody, including us, can recover them.
  • Links you open from the app (this policy, the FAQ, GitHub) open in your browser, under that site's own policy.

What stays on your device

  • Your Telegram session (in an encrypted database), the Telegram app keys and your encryption key, in the Keychain, readable only by Memvo on this device and never synced.
  • A local index of your library (names, dates, sizes, places and Telegram message IDs) so the timeline opens quickly. It is excluded from device backups because it can be rebuilt from Telegram.
  • Caches of thumbnails and items you viewed, with size limits you control.
  • A diagnostic log, which never leaves the device unless you share it yourself. Phone numbers, keys, channel IDs and file IDs are removed from it.

Settings → Sign out and reset deletes all of this from the device, and so does deleting the app. It doesn't delete your library in Telegram.

Permissions

  • Photos: to read the photos you choose to back up and to save downloaded ones back. You can allow limited access, or none, and still browse your cloud library.
  • Network: to talk to Telegram.

Children

Memvo isn't directed at children under 13, and it doesn't knowingly handle any child's data, because it handles no one's data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published here with a new effective date, and the history is visible in this repository.

Memvo is free software under the GPL-3.0. It uses the Telegram API and isn't made or endorsed by Telegram.
