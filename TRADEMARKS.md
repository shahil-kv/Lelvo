# Name and logo

The code is free: the [GPL-3.0](LICENSE) lets you use, study, change and share it. The **Memvo name and app
icon** are different. They tell people which app comes from this project, so nobody installs a copy that looks like
it but behaves differently.

## Without asking, you may

- Say that your project is **based on** or **a fork of** Memvo, and link here.
- Use the name in articles, reviews, tutorials and videos about the app.
- Use the name and icon unchanged when you redistribute an **unmodified** official release, with a link to this
  repository.

## Please don't

- Publish a modified version, or any app, under the name Memvo or a confusingly similar name, or with the
  Memvo icon. Give your fork its own name and icon.
- Publish anything with the name or icon on the App Store, Mac App Store, Google Play or any other store.
- Suggest that your project, service or business is official, endorsed by, or affiliated with Memvo.

If you're unsure, open an issue and ask.
