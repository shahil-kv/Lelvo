# About Lelvo

Lelvo backs up every photo and video from your iPhone or Mac, in original quality, to a private channel in your own
Telegram account. It shows what's on your device and what's in your Telegram library in one fast timeline.

## Why it exists

It's about ownership, not free storage. Your photos live in a channel you own, in an account you already have, and you
can open them without Lelvo. If Lelvo stopped tomorrow, your photos would still be yours.

## What it does

- **Original quality:** the same files you took, with their dates, places, albums, favourites and Live Photos.
- **One timeline:** phone and cloud items together, on iPhone and Mac, with a grid and viewer like Photos.
- **End-to-end encryption, always:** Lelvo encrypts each file on your device, so Telegram holds files it can't open.
- **Easy restore:** sign in on a new phone, pick your library, and everything is there.
- **Free up space:** remove photos from your phone only when you choose, and only after Lelvo checks they're safely
  backed up.

## How it works

Lelvo has no servers and no accounts of its own. It signs in to your Telegram account on your device and sends your
photos straight to your private channel. There are no analytics and no ads. The
[privacy policy](https://lelvo.app/privacy) has the details.

Lelvo uses the Telegram API and isn't made or endorsed by Telegram.
