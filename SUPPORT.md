# Getting help

Lelvo is a free, volunteer project, so the quickest answers are the ones already written down.

1. **Read the [FAQ](docs/FAQ.md).** It answers most questions: sign-in, backups that stop, deleted or frozen Telegram
   accounts, encryption, storage, restoring and sideloading.
2. **Search [existing issues](https://github.com/shahil-kv/Lelvo/issues?q=is%3Aissue)**, open and closed.
   Someone may have hit the same thing.
3. **Ask a question** in [Discussions → Q&A](https://github.com/shahil-kv/Lelvo/discussions/categories/q-a).
4. **Found a bug?** [Open a bug report](https://github.com/shahil-kv/Lelvo/issues/new?template=bug_report.yml)
   and attach the log (Settings → Logs → Share). Logs have phone numbers, keys and IDs removed, but read yours before
   you post it.
5. **Have an idea?** [Suggest a feature](https://github.com/shahil-kv/Lelvo/issues/new?template=feature_request.yml).
   Check [what's not planned](docs/not-planned.md) first.

**Security problems** never go in public issues: see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

**Your Telegram account itself** (banned, frozen, deleted, login codes) is Telegram's to fix. Lelvo can't
change anything about your account. See the [FAQ section](docs/FAQ.md#when-something-goes-wrong-in-telegram) and
[Telegram's support](https://telegram.org/support).
